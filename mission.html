Our mission

Recovery is worth representing with pride.

Dopeless Recovery Clothing is a recovery-inspired streetwear and lifestyle brand based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. We make clothing that stands for resilience, hope, personal growth — and the belief that people can change their lives.

We aren't trying to make addiction look fashionable.
We're making something positive out of a subject that has affected so many lives.

Who it's for

  • People in recovery and people rebuilding their lives.
  • People personally affected by addiction.
  • Families and friends who've lived through it.
  • Anyone who believes in second chances and positive change.
  • Streetwear fans who appreciate clothing with meaning.
